## # Hi, I'm Ahmed Wael Lethy 👋

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌾 Wheat Mitochondrial RNA Editing

A benchmarking study for identifying RNA editing sites in mitochondrial ATP synthase genes of wheat under drought stress.

**Topics:** RNA editing · mitochondrial genomics · variant calling · NGS · wheat · drought stress

### 🌾 Wheat Mitochondrial Comparative Genomics

Comparative analysis of wheat mitochondrial genomes including repetitive sequences, SSRs, tandem repeats, genomic similarity, annotation, and phylogenetic relationships.

**Topics:** comparative genomics · mitochondrial genomes · SSRs · BLAST · Circos · phylogeny

### 🦠 Metagenomics Analysis

**Topics:** microbiome · metagenomics · Kraken2 · HUMAnN · MEGAHIT · MAGs

### 💊 Molecular Docking & Drug Repurposing
